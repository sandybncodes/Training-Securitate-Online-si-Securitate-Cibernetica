## Timing suggestion

- Keep the pacing tight, especially in the experiments block.
- Prioritize Digital Detective, Social Engineering, Prompt Injection, and AI Upload Decisions.
- Use the optional exercises only if time allows.
- Leave S34 visible during the Q&A segment.

## Key slide cues

- S02: reveal the key statement only after the audience has responded.
- S03: external video is shown outside the app; avoid mentioning player controls or a file embed.
- S07: all five cards are emotional triggers; emphasize that they are examples, not proof.
- S10: the fake bank message should be discussed as a fictional simulation.
- S12: keep the card marked as editorial review until the source is verified.
- S18: the malicious instruction is intentionally synthetic and labeled as educational simulation.
- S30: frame the scenario as safeguarding and institutional response rather than blame.
- S31: the quiz is intentionally deterministic and does not collect personal data.
- S34: leave this slide on screen during Q&A and keep it as a final emotional anchor.

## Helpful presenter prompts

- „Ce emoție a fost folosită pentru a accelera decizia?”
- „Ce informație nu trebuie niciodată oferită fără verificare?”
- „Cum am putea verifica separat fără să bazăm totul pe mesajul primit?”
- „Ce alt canal de verificare exista?”
- „Ce riscuri apar dacă un copil trimite o fotografie sau un cod unui necunoscut?”

## Optional skip mechanism

If the session runs long, skip S25, S26, or the optional deepfake discussion in the AI chapter and move directly to the retention summary. The deck is structured to be flexible without breaking the flow.
